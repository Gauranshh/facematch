Privacy notice

This is the standard notice that ships with the FaceMatch software. Whoever runs this site should edit it (name, contact address, country-specific rights, retention) before inviting the public.

What this site does

FaceMatch lets you upload photos and then find the people in them. To make that possible, it detects faces in your photos and stores a numeric “face print” (an embedding) for each face, along with a small thumbnail of photos that contain faces.

Biometric data

Face prints are biometric data. They are used only to search your own photos. They are never used to identify people across different accounts, are not sold or shared, and are not used for advertising.

Only upload photos you have the right to use. If other people appear in them, make sure they are comfortable with that and that this is lawful where you live.

Who can see your photos

  • You, in your own account.
  • The site’s administrator, who runs the server and can technically access stored files.
  • Anyone you give a share link to, for the photos you choose, until the link expires or you revoke it.

Other people with accounts cannot see your photos, face prints or the people you name.

Your choices

  • Delete a photo at any time from My photos — the file, its thumbnail and its face prints are removed.
  • Delete your account from the account menu — this permanently removes your photos, face prints, saved people and share links.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Sessions use secure cookies. Photos are stored on the server’s disk and are not encrypted at rest unless the operator has enabled disk encryption.
